# IceHacks 2026: Hackathon Brief

Verified from the official Devpost page on Nov 15, 2026 (https://icehacks.devpost.com/), including the rules and prize sections.

## Event
- **Host:** CodeStarters, a student-led nonprofit (codestarters.org). Fully virtual.
- **Hacking period:** Sun Nov 8, 7:00 PM PT → **Sun Nov 15, 6:00 PM PT**
- **Deadline:** **Sun Nov 15, 2026, 6:00 PM PT = Mon Nov 16, 2026, 7:30 AM IST.** No late submissions.
- **Judging:** Nov 15 to Nov 17, 4:00 PM PT (asynchronous). Winners are announced Nov 17, 8:00 PM PT.

## Eligibility
- Open to **students**. The homepage names high school, college and middle school students. The rules say "Eligibility: 13+", and participants under 18 need guardian permission where required. College students **are** eligible.
- Teams of 1–4. Each person can be on only one team. Registration is through Devpost.
- All work must be started during the hackathon. Pre-existing code and significant third-party contributions must be disclosed.
- AI tools are permitted and encouraged, but significant AI-generated contributions **must be disclosed**.

## Judging criteria (five, equally weighted)
1. Technical Execution
2. Creativity & Originality
3. Impact & Usefulness
4. Design & User Experience
5. Presentation (demo video + write-up)

## Prize tracks
All prizes are credits, subscriptions and scholarships. The organizers state there are no cash prizes, even though Devpost shows cash-equivalent values. A project can opt into many tracks but **win only one**.

| Track | Prize highlights | Judged on |
|---|---|---|
| 1st / 2nd / 3rd Overall | SendCutSend credit, Scrimba Pro, CodeCrafters VIP | All five criteria |
| **Best AI Hack** | Momen credits, Anakin plan, $1,500 YRI scholarship | Usefulness, technical depth, polish, smart use of tools, impact |
| Best Hardware Hack | PCBWay credit | Hardware design (n/a for us) |
| **Best Design & UX** | Mobbin Pro | Usability, visual polish, accessibility, consistency |
| **Best Deployed / Shipped App** (1st–3rd) | DeployXA hosting credit | Working public URL, reliability, completeness, docs |

## Submission checklist (all required)
- [ ] **Demo video**: public YouTube/Vimeo/Youku link, **≤ 3 minutes**, showing the project working. It must explain the problem, show the demo and say how it was built. *Projects without a working video link are not judged.*
- [ ] **Write-up**: what it does, how it was built, challenges, what we learned, what's next
- [ ] **Code or live link**: public GitHub repo and/or live demo URL
- [ ] **Built with**: languages, frameworks, AI tools, sponsor tools
- [ ] **Tracks**: select every track the project fits
